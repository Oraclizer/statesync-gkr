# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Participation in StateSync-GKR should be respectful, technically rigorous, and
welcoming. Constructive behavior includes:

- critiquing claims and evidence without attacking people;
- stating assumptions, uncertainty, and conflicts of interest;
- respecting privacy, attribution, and intellectual-property rights;
- helping contributors understand review and verification requirements;
- accepting correction and maintainer decisions professionally.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory or sexualized
language, threats, doxxing, deliberate disclosure of private vulnerabilities,
personal attacks, sustained disruption, and retaliation against good-faith
reporting.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation, or
take other proportionate action when behavior threatens contributors, project
integrity, or security. Decisions consider context, impact, repetition, and
whether corrective direction was followed.

Report conduct concerns through the private contact channel in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not include unrelated personal data. Reports
are handled as confidentially as practical, but no absolute confidentiality
or response-time guarantee is made.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone represents the
project in public.
